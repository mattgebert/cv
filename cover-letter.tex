    % Knowledge and Skills
	I am writing to apply for the position of X-ray Senior Scientific Officer within the Monash X-ray Platform. As a postdoctoral researcher at Monash university with extensive experience in synchrotron-based X-ray spectroscopy and scattering, ultra-high vacuum instrumentation, user-training, experimental method developement and scientific software engineering, I am very excited by the opportunity to support and enable researchers in multi-disciplinary context through MXP's advanced characterisation capabilities. 

    What particularly attracts me to MXP is its role as an open-access research platform supporting researchers across disciplines and serving as a key complementary facility to the Australian Synchrotron. The opportunity to work directly with researchers, develop experimental methods, provide training and contribute to capability development is closely aligned with the aspects of research that I most enjoy.

    Across my academic career, I have participated in tutoring, training, outreach and mentoring activities, and I have a very strong interest in the relational aspects of research, and in enabling others to achieve their research goals. I have supervised multiple masters student project and actively contributed to PhD projects. I have both tutored and lab demonstrated in undergraduate physics and engineering courses, and have an aptitude for explaining complex concepts in a clear and accessible manner. 
    
    My education is quite diverse, and serves well to relate and support the variety of users that might work at the Monash X-ray Platform. 
    My current role is a postdoctoral research fellow under Prof. Chris McNeill in MSE at Monash University, with extensive experience in spectroscopy and diffraction beamline science which has been focused in utilising polarized light at resonance (NEXAFS and diffraction) to understand microstructure in thin-film organic semiconductors.
    Complementing my postdoc, I have a PhD in condensed matter physics under Prof. Michael Fuhrer within the FLEET Centre of Excellence, where I studied 2D electronic surfaces through the full electronic device lifecycle.
    My honours degree in electrical engineering is also particularly useful in the context of facility operation and control, as it has provided me with a strong understanding of electronics, instrumentation, and control systems.
    The breadth of my background demonstrates my ability to learn complex and new techniques to a high calibre, and I certainly would be eager to have the opportunity to work in the wide-spanning capabilities of the Monash X-ray Platform. 

    In addition to my user-oriented and scientific analytical skills, I have significant experience in community and governance roles, such as postgraduate represenatitive roles, music team coordination at a ~200 member church, and multiple other volunteering capacities. Some of these efforts have been recognised through awards, such as the Science Faculty Postraduate Community Award in 2020.  Through such roles I understand the importance of relationships with clients and stakeholders, how to create meaningful community connections and foster positive and inclusive environments. This record demonstrates my initiative, commitment and willingness to contribute meaningfully to professional communities. I am a strong team-player, and particularly work best when in collaboration with others. I believe that my experience and character demonstrated in research and community engagement has prepared me well to make strong contributions to the Monash X-ray Platform and its stakeholders, technical staff and researchers. 

    One particular aspect of my work that might be beneficial at the Monash X-ray Platform, and which isn't explicitly in the key selection criteria, is my abilities to create programmatic resources and documentation to enable users. My work has led to a strong interest in scientific software particularly oriented to reproducible user workflows. I have been developing open-source Python toolkits for experimental analysis of beamline experiments (see\cvreference{XraySoftMat}{https://github.com/xraysoftmat/}repositories). For example,\cvreference{`XEFI'}{https://github.com/xraysoftmat/XEFI}simulates the x-ray electric field intensity waveguided in thin films.
    I believe these skills are essential for modern research in user-focused facilities such as the Monash X-ray Platform, and particularly useful in the context of high-turnover x-ray tools, where repetitive dataset processing is required. I am confident that I am poised to contribute strongly to the development of new tools and workflows in conjunction with the platform to support user research.

    I hope you find my application promising and I look forward to an opportunity to discuss further. I am excited about the possibility of contributing to the platform, and I believe that my skills and experience align well with the requirements of this position.
