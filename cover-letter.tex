	I am writing to apply for the position of Controls Engineer as advertised through\cvreference{ANSTO (PD-2638)}{https://careers.ansto.gov.au/job/Melbourne-Controls-Engineer-VIC/1360799266/}for the Controls and Scientific Computing function. I am a postdoctoral research fellow in synchrotron science, with a physics doctorate from a cryogenic electronics lab and an undergraduate double degree in electrical and computer systems engineering and science. This opportunity is of great interest to me because of its multidisciplinary nature and long-term location in Melbourne, through which I believe I am ideally qualified for your team.

    \pind I have extensive experience beamline science, having completed 14 beamtimes. I have developed strong experimental experience in design and control aspects of in-situ WAXS experiments, for which I have been responsible within our group. I am familiar with control systems such as \cvreference{Bluesky}{https://github.com/bluesky/bluesky} (a modern open-source framework) built on top of EPICS, and EPICS formats such as MDA files, which are utilised for repeatable experimentation. In parallel, I have been developing open-source Python toolkits for interaction with and analysis of beamline experiments (see \cvreference{XraySoftMat}{https://github.com/xraysoftmat/} repositories). These tools contribute to reproducible, extensible, and integrated experimental workflows, and demonstrate strong software development capability to meet modern software-engineering standards (i.e. unit-testing, semantic versioning, etc.). I believe these skills are essential for modern control and automation research in user-focused facilities such as the Synchrotron.
    
    \pind My doctoral program and engineering electives have given me a strong background in electronic control and measurement systems. Most of my doctoral experiments were to conduct low-noise electronic measurements of 2D materials in strong magnetic fields. This requires a variety of electronic measurement equipment including lock-in amplifiers, sourcemeters and high-impedance preamplifiers, and their respective automated acquisition through GPIB LabView control. These measurements were often performed in cryogenic UHV environments employing control principles. I have also been required to implement safe operating procedures (SOPs) and risk assessments for such equipment, for the general use of other students and colleagues. Through educational electives, I have designed and implemented digital control loops for autonomous and time-critical tasks, such as a robotic puck competition, a PID-lock to a swinging pendulum, IQ-demodulation, digital clocks and real-time video-processors. I have implemented these systems on a variety of platforms, including microcontrollers, FPGAs and programmable system-on-chip (SoCs). I also have analogue circuit design experience, where I have implemented a custom differential-amplifier (Eagle), and done some introductory work with RF circuit design (i.e. Kesight ADS, LTSplice, Altium).

    \pind I have a strong capacity for detail, policy, and procedure, with significant experience in preparing standard operating procedures, risk assessments, and other safety documentation required in research contexts. The same is true for understanding compliance and best practice in engineering and scientific problem-solving, where details around specifications are critical. My diverse educational background also positions me to communicate effectively with a wide range of technical staff and researchers to deliver strong outcomes for the control and scientific computing group. While I have not been required to document design reviews in a control context explicitly, I have worked with significant amounts of CAD (AutoCAD) design and documentation, such as design documents for custom UHV parts and SOP documentation. 

    \pind My experience demonstrates excellent communication and interpersonal skills through a combination of collaboration, teaching, volunteering, and community activities. I have trained multiple PhD students in nanofabrication and measurement techniques, supervised numerous undergraduate and masters students, and taught X-ray analysis processes to PhD students in my research group. Through such roles, including community service and governance, I have learned the importance of relationships with clients and stakeholders, how to create meaningful community connections, and how to foster positive and inclusive environments. This record demonstrates my initiative in making relational contributions. I have demonstrated my ability to undertake self-directed learning in new fields that differ from my previous educational experience, and to collaborate with a wide range of researchers and staff. As a researcher, I have also internationally engaged with colleagues at European institutes, including recent ECR grant travel in Oct. 2025 through the ESRF and SLS.

    \pind I hope you find my application promising, and I look forward to the opportunity to discuss it further. I am excited about the possibility of contributing to this group and the broader synchrotron community, and I believe my skills and experience align well with the requirements of this position.
