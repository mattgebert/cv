    I am writing to apply for the position of Control Engineer at the Australian Synchrotron. This opportunity is of great interest to me because of its multidisciplinary nature, long-term location in Melbourne, at the intersection of scientific experimentation, electronic control, data and analysis, through all of which I believe I am ideally qualified to serve the research missions of the ANSTO. I have a strong and diverse background in engineering, physics, and software development, with extensive experience in scientific research and technical support. 

    My education includes a PhD in condensed matter physics within the School of Physics at Monash University focused on electronic materials, and  an honours degree in electrical and computer systems engineering majoring in digital (i.e. FPGA) and analogue electronics highlighted by the completion of two masters units; Real-time System Design\cvreference{(ECE5881)}{https://handbook.monash.edu/2026/units/ECE5881} and Advanced Analogue Electronics\cvreference{(ECE5882).}{https://handbook.monash.edu/2026/units/ECE5882}

    My postdoctoral research in synchrotron x-ray characterisation has enabled me to develop a deep understanding of control and detectors. In particular, I designed, constructed and implemented in-situ experiments using dynamic blade coating apparatuses, where controlled timing of motion was required to achieve dynamical speeds and imaging times at the SAXS/WAXS beamline. My work has required complex programmatic and data tasks to a industrial standard of software quality control, personally demonstrated through contributions to open source software development, particularly in Python, with multiple software packages published to the Python Package Index (PyPI), with user focussed documentation, and utilisation of programmatic AI models to progress software development.

    Through my research, I have demonstrated the ability to learn a wide range of technologies rapidly. I've extensively used electronic acquisition equipment (lock-ins, pre-amps), implemented control loops (LabVIEW, Python) and made significant use of cryogenic and UHV systems. My study has spanned relevant hardware programmatic languages, and engaged in multiple design projects that required me to design and build electronic systems. I have practical project experience across microcontrollers and FPGAs, including VHDL implementation, and PCB design and assembly, including soldering and testing of custom electronics, and wire-bonding for finer integration. I am also comfortable interacting at low level formats, such as the EPICS control layer with MDA formats.

    I have implemented safety documentation such as standard operating procedures and risk assessments for laboratory operations, and also have raised incident reports for equipment failures and near misses. I have performed this work across multiple facilities, including after-hours contexts, locally at the Monash X-ray Platform (MXP) and the ANFF Melbourne Centre for Nanofabrication (MCN), demonstrating my adherence to safety protocols across a range of environments. I have often worked in service and operational engagement with research and technical staff. I have great project management skills, working with overlapping deadlines and complex technical requirements, and utilized software project management toolkits such as Github Projects (with semantic versioning, unit testing, and continuous integration), which enable reliable interaction and feedback with a wider community. 

    I have excellent interpersonal and communication skills, exemplified in a variety of context.
    My work has enabled travel and collaboration with a wide range of researchers across international facilities and conferences, involving presentations and peer-reviewed publications. I also have significant teaching experience, and student mentoring in my research group, directly supervising four undergraduate/masters projects, and training multiple PhD students in nanofabrication and measurement techniques. My interpersonal skills are further highlighted by community service and governance roles.

    I believe my uniquely diverse experience makes me an ideal candidate for this position. With my passion for supporting people and enabling research, I am very excited to contribute to the controls group in this role. I look forward to the opportunity to discuss my application further.
