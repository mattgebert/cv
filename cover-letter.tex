As a postdoctoral researcher at Monash University with extensive experience in synchrotron-based X-ray spectroscopy and scattering, ultra-high vacuum instrumentation, user-training, experimental method development and scientific software engineering, I am very excited by the opportunity to support and enable researchers in a multi-disciplinary context through the Synchrotron's spectroscopy beamlines.

The opportunity to work directly with researchers, develop experimental methods, provide training and contribute to capability development is closely aligned with the aspects of research that I most enjoy.

I have focused in utilising polarized light at resonance (NEXAFS and diffraction) to understand microstructure in thin-film organic semiconductors. I have been responsible for planning, execution and reduction data from more than 15 synchrotron beamtimes within my research group in order to primarily support doctoral candidates. I have designed and undertaken a number of unique experiments at both MEX2 and SAXS/WAXS beamlines, as well as trained and mentored students in processes, equipment and subsequent analysis, which reflected in my publication record.
